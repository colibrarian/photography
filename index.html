About me

A little about who's behind the lens

Hi, I'm Taisiia
Film & portrait photographer · currently in Estonia

Photography is something that both relaxes and calms me — and at the same time energizes me and makes me want to shout to the world: notice this beauty too.

I love photographing everything — the sea, flowers, the light falling through trees on an ordinary street. But most of all, I adore photographing people.

This is more than work. Above all, it's my soul and my way of saying what words can't.
